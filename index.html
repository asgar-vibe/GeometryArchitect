Euclidean Construction Axioms:

Classical geometry permits only two ideal instruments: an unmarked Straightedge to connect points with straight lines, and a Compass to draw circles of congruent radii.

1. Law of Reflection & Hero's Theorem:

Light traverses the path of least time/distance. To reflect a ray from point A off a mirror plane to target B:
• Construct the virtual image B' symmetric to B across the mirror plane.
• Connect line segment AB'.
• The intersection with the mirror surface marks the exact point where angle of incidence equals angle of reflection (θ₁ = θ₂).

2. Circumcenter Construction (Perpendicular Bisectors):

The center of a circle circumscribing three non-collinear vertices lies at the concurrence point of their perpendicular bisectors:
• Using the Compass with equal radius from two vertices, draw intersecting arcs.
• Connect the intersections to form the perpendicular bisector of the chord.
• The intersection of two such bisectors is the Circumcenter O, equidistant to all three nodes.

3. Periscope & Corner Deflection (Dual Reflection):

Routing a beam around blast walls via two mirrors M1 and M2 requires consecutive virtual reflections:
• Reflect target T across M2 to find T'.
• Reflect T' across M1 to find T''.
• Aiming the emitter at T'' pinpoints the first reflection point on M1, which then automatically bounces off M2 into T!
